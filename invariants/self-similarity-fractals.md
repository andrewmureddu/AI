# Fractals & self-similarity

> **One-line claim:** Many natural structures and signals are statistically
> self-similar across scales, characterized by a non-integer fractal dimension.
> **Headline correspondence level:** L2 (shared geometric measure); L3 where a
> shared growth mechanism is identified. *(provisional — seed)*
> **Status:** seed

## Statement

A self-similar object looks statistically the same under magnification; its
Hausdorff/box-counting dimension D is non-integer. Domain-neutral object: the
fractal dimension D and self-affine scaling exponents (Hurst exponent H for time
series). Related to scale-free distributions (`power-laws.md`).

## Manifestations by domain (seed)

- Geophysics: coastlines, river networks, mountain profiles — L2.
- Biology: vascular trees, lungs, neurons — L3 (space-filling transport, WBE).
- Turbulence: energy cascade, Kolmogorov −5/3 spectrum — L3.
- Finance: rough volatility, fractional Brownian motion (H ≈ 0.1) — L2.
