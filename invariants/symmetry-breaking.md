# Symmetry & spontaneous symmetry breaking

> **One-line claim:** Systems with symmetric laws often settle into asymmetric
> states; the same "the ground state breaks the symmetry of the Hamiltonian"
> structure appears from particle physics to developmental biology.
> **Headline correspondence level:** L4 in physics; L2–L3 analogues elsewhere.
> *(provisional — seed)*
> **Status:** seed

## Statement

Spontaneous symmetry breaking (SSB): the dynamics are symmetric under a group G,
but the realized state is invariant only under a subgroup H ⊂ G. Goldstone modes
and order parameters follow. Domain-neutral object: G → H reduction with an order
parameter selecting one of degenerate ground states.

## Manifestations by domain (seed)

- Particle physics: Higgs mechanism, electroweak breaking — anchor, L4.
- Condensed matter: magnetization, crystallization, superconductivity — L4.
- Biology: left–right body-axis asymmetry, molecular chirality — L2/L3.
- Cosmology: baryogenesis, structure formation — L3.
