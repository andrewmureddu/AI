# Selection & replicator dynamics (universal Darwinism)

> **One-line claim:** Any population of imperfectly-copying variants under
> differential retention undergoes cumulative selection; the replicator equation
> describes evolution, learning, markets, and cultural change alike.
> **Headline correspondence level:** L2–L3; the "variation–selection–retention"
> algorithm is substrate-neutral, the equations sometimes coincide. *(seed)*
> **Status:** seed

## Statement

Replicator dynamics: ẋ_i = x_i (f_i − f̄), where x_i is the frequency of type i,
f_i its fitness, f̄ the mean. Domain-neutral object: the variation–selection–
retention loop and its replicator/Price-equation formalization. The Price equation
is an exact accounting identity for any selection process.

## Manifestations by domain (seed)

- Biology: natural selection — anchor.
- Economics: market selection of firms/strategies; evolutionary game theory — L3
  (replicator = fictitious play / exponential-weights limits).
- ML: genetic algorithms; multiplicative-weights / exponential-weights learning
  is *formally* replicator dynamics — L3.
- Culture: memetics — L1/L2 (units of selection ill-defined).
