# Emergence & levels of description

> **One-line claim:** Macroscopic behavior can be largely independent of
> microscopic detail; the renormalization group is the formal statement of *why*
> coarse-graining works and which micro-details survive.
> **Headline correspondence level:** L4 where RG applies; L1 as loose
> "emergence" talk. *(provisional — seed)*
> **Status:** seed
> **Floor:** **none — an operation on the tower**, not a fact inside it: RG carries Φ at one scale to Φ at another ([measured](../experiments/S25-channel-independence/))

## Statement

Coarse-graining integrates out short-scale degrees of freedom; RG flow shows most
microscopic couplings are *irrelevant* (flow to zero), leaving a few relevant
parameters that determine macro-behavior. This is the rigorous core behind
"emergence," "effective theories," and "more is different." Domain-neutral object:
the coarse-graining map and its relevant/irrelevant operator classification.

## Manifestations by domain (seed)

- Physics: thermodynamics from statistical mechanics; effective field theory — L4.
- ML: why over-parameterized models generalize; feature learning as RG (active
  research analogy) — L1/L2.
- Biology: robustness/canalization, genotype→phenotype maps — L1/L2.
- Social science: macro from micro (agent-based → aggregate) — L1.
