# Spectral gap (mixing, robustness, synchronization, consensus)

> **One-line claim:** The second eigenvalue of a system's operator governs how fast
> it mixes, how robustly it stays connected, how quickly it reaches consensus, and
> whether it synchronizes — the same number across all four.
> **Headline correspondence level:** **L3** — one mechanism (the slow mode), with
> **family-specific constants**; the "one number" reading is *not* supported.
> **Status:** developing ✓tested ([P-A](../experiments/PA-spectral-gap/))
> **Floor:** **3** (Φ-singular) — **confirmed on evidence**, in the *directional*
> form: gap-closure is a **null direction** of the trajectory free energy's
> Hessian, not a scalar divergence ([P-A](../experiments/PA-spectral-gap/))

## Statement

For a Markov/Laplacian operator, the spectral gap (1 − second eigenvalue, or the
Fiedler value λ₂ of the graph Laplacian) sets the slowest relaxation mode.
Domain-neutral object: the spectral gap and the time/threshold scales it controls.

## Manifestations by domain (seed)

- CS: MCMC mixing time ~ 1/gap; expander graphs; PageRank convergence. L3.
- Physics: relaxation time to equilibrium ~ 1/gap. L3.
- Networks: algebraic connectivity (Fiedler) = robustness to disconnection. L3.
- Multi-agent/social: consensus convergence rate = λ₂. L2–L3.
- Neuro/physics: Kuramoto synchronization threshold tied to spectrum. L2.

## Why it's here / to develop

Underlies robustness/mixing/consensus/sync questions. The portable prediction: one
measured gap predicts mixing time, consensus speed, and sync threshold together.
Falsifier: a system where these decouple from the gap. Cross-links
`networks-percolation.md`. Refs: Fiedler 1973; Levin–Peres; Chung (spectral graph).

## Result (2026-07-26) — tested, and the "one number" reading demotes

[`../experiments/PA-spectral-gap/`](../experiments/PA-spectral-gap/). This entry
was assigned to floor 3 by the [floor sort](./FLOORS.md) with no evidence; that
prediction was then run.

**First, a correction to how this entry states its own claim.** For the walk
generated by `Q = −L`, three of the four readouts — mixing rate, consensus rate,
algebraic connectivity — are *the same eigenvalue by construction*. Their
agreement was never evidence for anything. Only the synchronization threshold is
free to disagree.

- **The gap carries the free energy's divergence.** With Φ fixed in advance as the
  SCGF of a time-averaged observable, the slowest mode comes to carry the entire
  divergence of Λ''(0) (slow-mode fraction → 1.0000; Λ''·gap = 2⟨f,v₂⟩² = 2.0000).
- **But the divergence is directional.** For an observable orthogonal to the slow
  mode, Λ''(0) is *unchanged* (1.00×) while the gap falls 31×. The entry belongs
  with null-direction phenomena, not with scalar susceptibility blow-up.
- **Sync: one mechanism, family-specific constants.** K_c·λ₂ tends to a constant
  within each topology, but the constant spans 0.410–0.632 across topologies.
  The gap fully absorbs *bridge multiplicity* (1 bridge and 5 bridges give 0.562
  identically) and does **not** absorb *size asymmetry* (8+22 gives 0.410).
- **Boundary — and it costs an existing result.** τ·λ = 1 held to 1.000 across 28
  graph configurations, and failed by up to five orders of magnitude in a double
  well, where the gap falls 3540× while the local curvature *rises* 6×. The
  relevant λ is the trajectory free energy's, not the potential's. This is a new
  boundary condition on [S7](../experiments/S7-critical-slowing/): **its law is a
  single-basin law.**
