# P-A — is the spectral gap's closure the ∇²Φ degeneracy? (result)

**Prediction under test:** [`invariants/FLOORS.md`](../../invariants/FLOORS.md) §3.
The floor sort put the [spectral-gap entry](../../invariants/spectral-gap.md) (#19)
on floor 3 with **no evidence at all**, predicting: gap-closure *is* the ∇²Φ
degeneracy, #19's four readouts are one number, and the whole thing joins
[S7](../S7-critical-slowing/)'s τ·λ_min = 1 from the connectivity side.

**Status: floor assignment confirmed — but in the *directional* form, not the
scalar one, and it corrects an existing repo result rather than joining it.**

Run it: `python3 run.py` (pure numpy, deterministic, ~6 s). Predictions were
registered in [`predictions.json`](./predictions.json) before the run.

## Design

Two commitments were made in writing before looking, because both were ways the
experiment could have graded itself:

1. **Φ is fixed in advance.** Φ = Λ(k), the scaled cumulant generating function
   of a time-averaged observable — the top eigenvalue of the tilted generator
   `Q + k·diag(f)`. This is a genuine log-partition function (of the trajectory
   ensemble), and Λ''(0) is the asymptotic variance. No post-hoc choice of Φ was
   permitted.
2. **Three of the four readouts cannot count as evidence.** For the walk
   generated by `Q = −L`, the mixing rate, the consensus rate and the algebraic
   connectivity are *the same eigenvalue by construction*. Only the Kuramoto
   threshold — a nonlinear dynamical quantity, free to disagree — tests the "one
   number" claim.

Legs: **A** bottleneck graphs (four topologies × seven bottleneck strengths);
**B** an observable orthogonal to the slow mode; **C** a double well, where the
gap closes by barrier crossing rather than by disconnection.

## Results

### The gap does carry the free energy's divergence — in one direction only

| Prediction | Result |
|---|---|
| **P1** Λ''(0) two independent ways (tilted-generator finite difference vs. spectral sum) | ✓ agree to **7.5e-8** |
| **P2** the slow mode alone comes to carry the whole divergence | ✓ slow-mode fraction → **1.0000** (two-clique), 0.9969 (ring); Λ''·gap = 2⟨f,v₂⟩² = **2.0000** |
| **P3** for f ⊥ v₂, Λ''(0) stays **bounded** | ✓ Λ'' = **0.0192, unchanged (1.00×)** while the gap fell **31×** |

P3 is the one that matters. The gap can close by any amount and the free energy's
second derivative *does not move* unless the observable overlaps the slow mode. So
"gap-closure is the Hessian degenerating" is true as a **null-direction**
statement and false as a statement about a number. That places the spectral gap
with [S25(K)](../../derivations/S25-control-split.md)'s unreachable null spaces
and the Goldstone flat directions rather than with S3's scalar divergence — and
it **demotes the scalar reading of P-A as I wrote it.**

### The one readout free to disagree: mechanism transfers, constant doesn't

K_c is found by numerical continuation of the locked state (cross-checked against
direct integration: continuation gives K_c = 33.456; simulation locks at 1.15·K_c
and fails to lock at 0.85·K_c).

| Family | asymptotic K_c·λ₂ |
|--------|------------------:|
| two cliques, 1 bridge | **0.562** |
| two cliques, 5 bridges | **0.562** |
| weak ring | 0.632 |
| asymmetric cliques (8+22) | **0.410** |

Within a family K_c·λ₂ tends to a constant — flat to ≤8% over a 64× range of
bottleneck strength for three of four families; the ring converges more slowly
(13% over its small-gap half) because its gap has two competing contributions
until ε is small enough for the weak links to dominate.

Two things worth separating. The gap **completely absorbs** bridge multiplicity —
one bridge and five bridges give *the same* constant to three digits. It does
**not** absorb size asymmetry: 8+22 sits 27% below 15+15. So the gap is
necessary and nearly sufficient, but the sync threshold retains a topological
dependence the single number does not capture.

**A registered threshold I got wrong, on the record.** I predicted the
across-family constants would differ by more than 2×. The asymptotic spread is
**1.54×**. The prediction is directionally right — one mechanism, family-specific
constants, the same shape as [S7](../S7-critical-slowing/) and
[S5](../S5-noise-thresholds/) — but I overstated the magnitude, and the >2.0
version passes only if you include a data point that has not converged. Scored on
asymptotic constants, it fails as written.

### The double well: the naive reading dies, the tower reading survives

| h | gap | U″(±1) | S7's τ·λ_min | Λ''·gap |
|--:|----:|-------:|-------------:|--------:|
| 0.5 | 1.14e-1 | 4 | 3.5e1 | 1.633 |
| 1.0 | 2.98e-2 | 8 | 2.7e2 | 1.769 |
| 2.0 | 1.14e-3 | 16 | 1.4e4 | 1.893 |
| 3.0 | 3.21e-5 | 24 | 7.5e5 | 1.932 |

**The gap falls 3540× while the local curvature rises 6×** — they move in
*opposite directions*. So the falsifier as literally stated ("mixing time diverges
while the relevant Hessian stays gapped") **fires**, against any reading that
identifies the gap with a local curvature.

The tower reading survives with the Φ fixed in advance: Λ''·gap stays O(1) and
climbs to 1.93, converging to the two-state value 2. The Hessian that degenerates
is the **trajectory free energy's**, not the potential's.

## Verdict

- **#19's floor-3 assignment: confirmed, now on evidence.** The entry moves from
  "the sort's prediction" to a tested claim — but the claim that survives is
  directional: gap-closure is a *null direction* of the trajectory free energy's
  Hessian.
- **#19's "one number" reading: demoted to L3, one mechanism with
  family-specific constants.** Three readouts were one number by construction and
  never tested anything; the fourth tracks 1/λ₂ with a topology-dependent
  prefactor spanning 1.54×.
- **A boundary condition for an existing result — S7's law is a single-basin
  law.** τ·λ_min = 1 holds to **1.000** across all 28 graph configurations here,
  and fails by **up to five orders of magnitude** in the double well. S7's three
  legs (saddle-node, mean-field Ising, GD at the MP edge) are all single-basin, so
  the law was never exposed to barrier crossing. Restated: τ·λ = 1 where the slow
  mode *is* a local curvature; where relaxation is barrier crossing, the correct
  λ is the trajectory free energy's, not the potential's.
- **Not a sixth arrival at the ∇²Φ singular set — a correction to the fourth.**
  It would be easy to bank this as another independent arrival. It isn't one: it
  is the S7 arrival, sharpened (the right Hessian) and made directional (P3). The
  honest effect on the [synthesis](../../SYNTHESIS.md)'s arrivals table is to
  tighten arrival 4, not to add a row.

## Boundaries

- Reversible chains with uniform π (`Q = −L`) and one 1-D double well. Nothing
  here tests non-reversible generators, where the gap is not the only relaxation
  scale and eigenvalues go complex.
- The Kuramoto threshold is found by continuation from the high-K branch, so it
  is that branch's saddle-node; hysteresis and other locked branches are not
  explored. Frequencies are one fixed draw (seed 7) shared across families, so
  the constants are not averaged over frequency disorder.
- Four topologies is enough to show the constant varies, not enough to
  characterize *how* it varies with topology.
- The double well is 1-D with a detailed-balance discretization; the gap is
  resolved down to 3.2e-5, which bounds how deep a barrier can be probed before
  float64 eigenvalues stop being trustworthy.
- P2's slow-mode fraction is exactly 1 only asymptotically; where the slow
  subspace is near-degenerate (the ring, with two weak links) a single mode does
  not carry the divergence until the degeneracy splits.
