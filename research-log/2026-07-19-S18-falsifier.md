# 2026-07-19 — Restraint: the prediction-field falsifier survived

**Worked on:** S18 / the prediction-field frame's central falsifier
**Change:** new experiment; frame's 🟢 core lifted from "true by definition" to
"demonstrated to bite"

## Why this one

The prediction-field frame is the repo's spine, and its *only* stated falsifier is
S18: does invariance-across-environments actually predict transfer? Running the
falsifier of the most load-bearing assumption is the highest-value restraint act
available — if it fired, the whole frame reorganizes.

## What I did

Built a linear SCM ([`../experiments/S18-invariance-transfer/`](../experiments/S18-invariance-transfer/),
pure numpy, deterministic) with an invariant causal feature (X_c → Y, fixed
coefficient) and a spurious correlate (X_s = b_e·Y, coupling varies by environment).
Trained on `b_e ∈ {0.8,1,1.2,1.5}`; tested on a held-out `b = −1.2` (out of range,
sign-flipped). Measured invariance on *training* envs and transfer on the *test*
env — disjoint data, so the relationship isn't a tautology.

## What I found

- **The inversion:** spurious feature is 6× better in-distribution (MSE 0.16 vs
  0.99) but 8× worse on transfer (7.71 vs 0.95). ERM, seduced by the spurious
  feature, transfers badly (6.56).
- **Invariance ⇒ transfer, ρ = 0.985:** training-set coefficient instability almost
  perfectly ranks held-out transfer error. The prediction-field claim, confirmed
  operationally.
- **Blind selection works:** ICP-style "pick the most stable coefficient across
  training envs" recovers X_c without seeing the test env.

## Verdict + honest limits

Falsifier **did not fire**. The frame's 🟢 operational core moves from "true by
definition" to **"true and demonstrated to bite"** — the most in-distribution-
predictive signal really is the wrong one to trust. Crucially this does **not**
promote the 🔴 metaphysical reading, and invariance = causation was baked into the
SCM by construction. Recorded in [`../PREDICTION-FIELD.md`](../PREDICTION-FIELD.md)
and the S18 entry.

## The restraint that this restraint owes

The constructed-SCM caveat is the whole open question. The *real* test:
- Take 3–4 catalog invariants at different ladder levels and measure genuine A→B
  predictive transfer across actually-different domains. Only that moves the frame
  from self-consistent to empirically load-bearing.
- Cheaper intermediate: nonlinear / anti-causal SCM to map where invariance⇒transfer
  weakens.

## Loop status

Three full expand⇄restrain cycles now on record: S1 (derive) → hub + S13–S18
(expand) → S18 (restrain). Two experiments and one derivation have teeth; the
prediction-field spine has survived its first real test.
