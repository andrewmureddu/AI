## What I did

Ran the first of the [floor sort](../invariants/FLOORS.md)'s predictions —
**P-A**, the claim that gap-closure *is* the ∇²Φ degeneracy, asserted about an
entry that had never been tested.

Two commitments were written down before looking, because both were ways the
experiment could have graded itself:

1. **Φ fixed in advance** as Λ(k), the scaled cumulant generating function of a
   time-averaged observable — a genuine log-partition function of the trajectory
   ensemble. Without this the tower reading is unfalsifiable, since one can
   always find *some* Φ after the fact.
2. **Three of #19's four readouts cannot count as evidence.** For the walk
   generated by Q = −L, mixing rate, consensus rate and algebraic connectivity are
   the same eigenvalue *by construction*. Only the Kuramoto threshold is free to
   disagree. Registering this stopped the entry's headline claim from being
   confirmed by a tautology.

## What I found

**The floor assignment holds — in the directional form only.** The slow mode comes
to carry the entire divergence of Λ''(0) (slow-mode fraction → 1.0000;
Λ''·gap = 2⟨f,v₂⟩² = 2.0000). **But for an observable orthogonal to the slow mode,
Λ''(0) does not move at all** (1.00×) while the gap falls 31×. So "gap-closure is
the Hessian degenerating" is true as a *null-direction* statement and false as a
statement about a number. That files #19 with S25(K)'s unreachable null spaces and
the Goldstone flat directions, not with S3's scalar susceptibility blow-up — and
it demotes the scalar reading of P-A as I wrote it.

**Kuramoto: one mechanism, family-specific constants.** K_c·λ₂ tends to a constant
within each topology; across topologies the constants are 0.562 (1 bridge), 0.562
(5 bridges), 0.632 (weak ring), 0.410 (asymmetric 8+22). Two separable facts: the
gap **completely absorbs bridge multiplicity** — one bridge and five give the same
constant to three digits — and it does **not** absorb size asymmetry. So the gap is
necessary and nearly sufficient, with a residual topological dependence.

**A registered threshold failed as written, and it is on record.** I predicted the
across-family spread would exceed 2×. On asymptotic constants it is **1.54×**. The
>2.0 version passes only if you include a ring data point that has not converged.
Directionally right, magnitude overstated — the third instance of the S7/S5 shape
(mechanism transfers, constants don't), but the mildest of the three.

**The cost: S7's law is a single-basin law.** In a double well the spectral gap
falls **3540×** while the local potential curvature U″(±1) **rises 6×** — opposite
directions — so S7's τ·λ_min product runs from 3.5e1 to 7.5e5 instead of staying
at 1. All three of S7's legs (saddle-node, mean-field Ising, GD at the MP edge)
relax *within* a basin, so the law was never exposed to barrier crossing. Taking λ
from the trajectory free energy instead restores it (Λ''·gap → 2). This is the
falsifier P-A itself listed ("mixing diverges while the relevant Hessian stays
gapped") firing against the naive reading.

**Not a sixth arrival.** It would have been easy to bank this as another
independent arrival at the ∇²Φ singular set and grow the synthesis's headline
count from five to six. It is not one — it is the *fourth* arrival (S7),
sharpened into the right Hessian and made directional. The synthesis's arrivals
table was tightened rather than extended.

## Decisions

- #19's headline level set at **L3**: one mechanism (the slow mode limits
  synchronization), family-specific constants. The "one number across four
  readouts" reading is not supported and the entry now says why — three of the
  four were never independent.
- The entry's own framing was corrected, not just its level. An entry whose
  evidence is three definitionally-equal numbers is mis-stated, however true its
  conclusion.
- S7's stone and #22 both carry the new boundary. S7's headline result stands; its
  scope shrank.
