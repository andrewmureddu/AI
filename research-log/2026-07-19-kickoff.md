## What I found (framing choices, not yet results)

The five developed entries were chosen to span the ladder deliberately:

- **Diffusion / random walks** and **Conservation / Noether** are the cleanest
  **L4** anchors — each rests on a *theorem* (CLT; Noether), so the cross-domain
  coincidence is forced, not observed.
- **Criticality / universality** is the deepest genuinely surprising case (Ising ≡
  liquid–gas exponents) *and* the most abused word outside physics — a perfect
  illustration of why one invariant needs an internal L4-core / L1-fringe line.
- **Entropy / information** shows an **L3** bridge (MaxEnt) that decays to L2/L1 as
  you leave a well-defined state space.
- **Power laws** shows the opposite trap: one **L2** shape hiding several distinct
  **L3** mechanisms, heavily over-claimed in the literature.

Early read from the matrix: physics is the anchor column, and the central research
question for almost every other cell is *inherited-from-physics vs. native*.
