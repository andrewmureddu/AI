# 2026-07-19 — Restraint: attention is the universal update (S23, exact)

## What I did

Derived, then verified numerically
([`../experiments/S23-attention-bayes/`](../experiments/S23-attention-bayes/), pure
numpy): softmax attention over memories (kᵢ, vᵢ) equals the Bayesian posterior over
"which memory generated the query" under `q|z=i ~ N(kᵢ, σ²I)`, which is S1's
universal update `xᵢ ∝ xᵢ·exp(−η gᵢ)` applied once from a uniform prior, with loss
gᵢ = ½‖q−kᵢ‖² and step η = 1/σ².

## What I found

1. **Exact to machine precision.** With norm-equalized keys and σ²=√d, attention
   weights = Bayes posterior to 1.4e-17; outputs to 6e-17.
2. **Attention temperature = η.** Sweeping β, prediction MSE bottoms out at
   β = 0.501 vs the Bayes-optimal 1/σ0² = 0.500. The temperature is the noise
   precision, not a free knob.
3. **Key-norm bias (bonus finding).** *Plain* dot-product attention is a *biased*
   posterior — it drops the ‖kᵢ‖²/2σ² term, implicitly boosting high-norm keys.
   Adding the term restores exactness (1e-17). This *explains why* QK-normalization
   and cosine attention exist — the stone didn't just confirm, it accounted for an
   existing engineering practice.
